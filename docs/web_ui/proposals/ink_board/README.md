# Proposal: the board drawn in ink

A proposal from the visual audit of 2026-11-12, **not approved**: the author is collecting opinions. The
approved look of the board is `../../references/battle_mockup/` (fog: `fog_b.png`).

- `today.png`: the Pit as the game draws it today (from `docs/photos/12112026/`).
- `ink_board.png`: the same fight and the same interface, with the board as a plan inked on paper.
- `ink_board.html`: the page that draws it, over `today.png`. Open it in Edge at 1920x1080 with
  `--allow-file-access-from-files`.

What changes: the floor is no longer a texture but a survey drawn on the sheet, like the world map (hatched
walls and posts, stippled sand); colour is what the squad sees now (a warm wash by a torch, a cool one in the
dark); what was seen before is ink with no colour; what was never seen is empty paper; the sheet fills the
whole panel and carries a title, a scale, a compass and a note in the margin; tokens are pieces with a
shadow on the sheet.

Not tried: a dungeon with corridors, the wash's edge at a finished quality, the marks of the real board
(conditions, items, bodies).
